Colors that speak

Design philosophy

The interface uses a small set of high-signal colors so important states are easy to recognize at a glance.

Green: Safe speed

Indicates the app’s normal driving state and primary actions.

Amber: Caution

Signals that the configured threshold is approaching and attention is needed.

Red: Alert

Marks an over-threshold state or another action requiring immediate attention.
