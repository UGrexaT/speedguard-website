What it does

A conservative driving aid.

SpeedGuard uses device location while monitoring is active to estimate vehicle speed, look up available road speed-limit data, and provide alerts based on the user's settings.

SpeedGuard is not a substitute for posted signs, road conditions, vehicle instruments, driver judgment, or traffic laws. It is informational only.

App facts

Built around awareness, not automation.

Speed and alerts

The Android app estimates speed from location readings and can alert when the estimated speed exceeds a configured threshold.

Speed-limit lookup

The app sends location-derived road queries to SpeedGuard services for speed-limit lookup. Premium users may receive additional map-provider fallback checks.

Free and premium access

The Android app includes a free tier with ads and RevenueCat-backed monthly or yearly SpeedGuard Pro subscription handling for premium access.

Important limits

Always drive by the road in front of you.

Safety notice: Speed limit, location, map, and alert information may be inaccurate, outdated, delayed, unavailable, or incomplete. Users remain responsible for obeying traffic laws and driving safely.
