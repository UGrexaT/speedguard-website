What it does

A focused driving companion.

Vehicle Speeding Guard uses device location while monitoring is active to estimate vehicle speed, look up available road speed-limit data, and provide alerts based on the user's settings.

Vehicle Speeding Guard is not a substitute for posted signs, road conditions, vehicle instruments, driver judgment, or traffic laws. It is informational only.

App facts

Built around awareness, not automation.

The app keeps the main driving surface uncluttered: status at the top, speed in the center, controls at the bottom.

Live speed and alerts

The Android app estimates speed from location readings and can alert when the estimated speed exceeds a configured threshold.

Speed-limit lookup

The app sends location-derived road queries to Vehicle Speeding Guard services for speed-limit lookup. Premium users may receive additional map-provider fallback checks.

Free and premium access

The Android app includes a free tier with ads and RevenueCat-backed monthly or yearly Vehicle Speeding Guard Pro subscription handling for premium access.

Android design

Dark, glanceable, and built for motion.

The product UI uses a dark cockpit palette, large tabular speed numbers, status pills for GPS and tracking state, and simple bottom actions for monitoring and upgrades.
