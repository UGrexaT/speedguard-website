Highway 401 speed limits 2026

The 401, section by section

Since August 31, 2026, the only part of the 401 still at 100 km/h is central Toronto, from Highway 427 to Highway 404. There are no variable (electronic) speed limits on the 401 or the QEW, so the number on the sign is the limit.

Ontario’s 110 km/h expansion

By September 30, 2026, 110 km/h covered about 89% of Ontario’s provincial highway network (1,811 km), up from 43%. Between June and September 2026, 938 km were raised, including sections of the QEW and Highways 400, 401, 402, 403, 404, 406, 407, 412, 416, 417 and 418.

Not every section changed, and the changes are recent, so watch the signs on any highway you haven’t driven lately. Where no limit is posted, Ontario’s default outside built-up areas is 80 km/h.

150 km/h is stunt driving everywhere

Ontario treats 150 km/h or more as stunt driving on any road, including 110 km/h freeways, where that’s only 40 over. At the roadside that means an immediate 30-day licence suspension and a 14-day vehicle impoundment, even if it isn’t your car.

If convicted: a $2,000–$10,000 fine (or up to 6 months in jail), 6 demerit points and a 1–3-year licence suspension for a first conviction.

The province’s own figures explain why: in 2013–2017 Ontario data, the risk of death or serious injury was 11 times higher in collisions at 50 km/h or more over a 100 km/h limit than at or below the limit.

The fuel bill goes the other way: Natural Resources Canada says a car uses about 20% more fuel at 120 km/h than at 100.

Stopping and following distance

On a dry road, an average car needs about 98 m to stop from 100 km/h and 113 m from 110 (122 m and 143 m on a wet road), according to the Queensland Government’s stopping-distance data. Keep at least a 2-second gap to the car ahead (MTO Driver’s Handbook): about 56 m at 100 km/h.

Can G1 drivers use the 401?

No. G1 drivers can’t drive on 400-series highways or high-speed expressways unless the supervising driver is a certified driving instructor. G1 drivers also can’t drive between midnight and 5 a.m.

Questions people ask

What is the speed limit on Highway 401?

110 km/h from Windsor to Highway 427 and from Highway 404 to the Quebec border. In central Toronto, between Highways 427 and 404, it is still 100 km/h.

Is the 401 110 km/h in Toronto?

Not in central Toronto. Between Highway 427 and Highway 404 the limit is still 100 km/h. East of the 404 it is 110 km/h.

Is the 401 110 km/h in Mississauga?

Yes. The Mississauga stretch of the 401 is 110 km/h.

When did the 401 become 110 km/h between Windsor and Toronto?

The increase between Windsor and Toronto took effect on August 31, 2026. Since then, the only part of the 401 still at 100 km/h is central Toronto, between Highways 427 and 404.

Are there variable speed limits on the 401?

No. There are no variable (electronic) speed limits on the 401 or the QEW.

What speed is stunt driving on the 401?

150 km/h or more counts as stunt driving on any Ontario road. On a 110 km/h section of the 401 that is only 40 over. In the 100 km/h section, 50 over (150 km/h) is also stunt driving.

Can G1 drivers drive on the 401?

No. G1 drivers cannot drive on 400-series highways or high-speed expressways unless the supervising driver is a certified driving instructor.
